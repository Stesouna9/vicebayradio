Its story

Russell opened in 1976 with five cars and tinted glasses. He judges saloons by their seats and estate cars by the number of beach bags they hold.

In 1984, the red model stopped pedestrians outside the window. Russell made it his star, then painted a floor line so admiration would leave room for other customers.

Anecdotes

  • In April 1984, customer Colin Wade brought nine bags to measure a boot.
  • In November 1985, Mél announced a three-hour silent test drive; Russell showed her his three-minute timer.
  • In May 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the finance desk and requested a chair easier than the paperwork.
