Its story

Leonard opened in 1963 after cutting his three brothers’ hair in a kitchen. He keeps the first mirror; its missing corner conveniently hides the till.

The heat of 1984 flattened blow-dries before customers reached the pavement. He fitted an adjustable fan and introduced a “lasting to the car” finish alongside his classic cuts.

Anecdotes

  • In April 1984, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the fan pointed at her own hair.
  • In August 1985, Gabriel tapped the armrest in rhythm; Leonard waited for the brass to finish before trimming his neck.
  • In January 1986, customer Cecil Rusk brought $12.99 and a cardboard beard; Leonard refused the discount.
