Its story

Edmund opened in 1970 with two chairs and forms filed by thickness. He explains loans and applications while allowing customers time to read the small print.

In 1985, the clock began losing five minutes a day. Edmund kept it but added a watch on the counter; appointments stay punctual while the décor takes its time.

Anecdotes

  • In November 1984, customer Vernon Cole brought a magnifier and signed only the coffee receipt.
  • In December 1985, Big Lou tapped a file twice like his cassettes; Edmund checked the staples.
  • In March 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the second chair, already occupied for sixteen years.
