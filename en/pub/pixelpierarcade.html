Its story

Beatrice and her son Calvin opened in 1980 with six cabinets and a stool. She repairs coin mechanisms; he records racing, space-game and brick-breaking scores.

In 1985, cabinet eight displayed ZAP and 900,000 points. Nobody can identify the player; a written inquiry remains unanswered. Visitors come to verify the score and fund two more cabinets.

Anecdotes

  • In July 1984, customer Lorna Beck spent her coins comparing the two racing machines’ steering wheels.
  • In November 1985, Melvin sent a beep readout; Calvin refused to count it as a game.
  • In April 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated a ticket roll without breaking any records.
