Its story

Gordon opened in 1973 with a second-hand lift and a noise notebook. He repairs brakes, engines, batteries and air conditioning with his eyes fully open.

In 1985, a customer asked whether a free diagnosis included three hours of dismantling. Gordon made two columns: free listening, technical investigation from $29. He shows them before taking the keys.

Anecdotes

  • In June 1984, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the lift without standing on it.
  • In October 1985, Mél brought a noise recorded on cassette; Gordon identified her indicator.
  • In April 1986, customer Perry Holt imitated an engine for nine minutes; Gordon offered him water.
