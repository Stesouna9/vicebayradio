Its story

Otis and his sister Pearl opened in 1968 after towing three neighbours in one day. He repairs engines and air conditioning; she drives the recovery truck and chooses firm tracks.

In 1984, a shoe jammed an engine. Pearl removed it, the engine started and the customer recommended the garage to eleven cousins; the shoe’s owner remains unknown.

Anecdotes

  • In September 1984, customer Wade Pell recognised the size but refused to recognise the shoe.
  • In January 1985, Big Lou brought a hot cable; Otis reminded him the rate covered cars.
  • In May 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the recovery hook resting on a cushion.
