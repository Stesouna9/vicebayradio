Its story

Irene and Paul opened in 1958 with ten rooms and a breakfast promise. They withdrew the promise on day two and expanded to nineteen rooms.

In 1984, a leak emptied the pool. Repairs would cost six months’ takings; Paul repainted the bottom and Irene bought mattresses. Drivers returned for the sleep.

Anecdotes

  • In October 1984, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the lit car park with an unlit torch.
  • In February 1985, Marie left a dedication in the register; Régis added a paw print.
  • In June 1986, customer Owen Pike booked room 19 for his palm and received the room overlooking another one.
