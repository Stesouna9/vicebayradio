Its story

Vernon and Ada opened in 1959 with three wrecks and a box of sorted bolts. He removes engines and doors; she files wheels, seats and mirrors by compatibility.

In 1984, an old radio left outdoors for twenty years started playing again. A customer offered $300; Vernon refused. Ada moved it under a roof without removing it from inventory.

Anecdotes

  • In July 1984, customer Lyle Bost offered his $300 during a jazz solo; Vernon waited until it ended to refuse.
  • In December 1985, Big Lou heard the radio and called its bass line insolent; Ada recorded “reception correct”.
  • In August 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the mirror section and appeared in twelve photographs at once.
