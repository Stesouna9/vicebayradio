Its story

Alma has made sandwiches and salads since 1969; Ruben opens coconuts with a tool he calls his secretary. Their first counter was 1.8 metres long.

In 1984, wind carried off the menus but left the coconuts standing. Ruben introduced two for one; Alma insisted customers see the smaller one before paying.

Anecdotes

  • In May 1984, customer Nora Pell returned a coconut marked “reserve” and asked since when.
  • In July 1985, Gabriel borrowed two shells as percussion instruments; Ruben required their return washed.
  • In March 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the ice bin, full of juice because Ruben had read too quickly.
