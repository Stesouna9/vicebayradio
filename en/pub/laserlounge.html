Its story

Vincent and Paula opened in 1983 with a shiny floor and four screens. She programmes lights; he measures reflections on shoes before opening the doors.

In 1985, their new laser arrived with its manual’s last page missing. Paula covered the red button; its reputation attracted curious people who danced while awaiting an explanation.

Anecdotes

  • In June 1984, customer Dean Moss brought matt shoes and demanded their share of reflection.
  • In October 1985, Melvin’s test cassette helped time the flashes, without using his voice in an advertisement.
  • In May 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the red button’s cover; Paula kept the key.
