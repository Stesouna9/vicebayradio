Its story

Frank started in 1954 with one taxi and a towel for wet seats. Elsie organises calls on cards in three colours, all meaning urgent.

The 1984 works closed his favourite route along 3e Avenue. Elsie drew a Front Street diversion; Frank still calls it a shortcut even when the east bridge stays raised.

Anecdotes

  • In October 1984, customer Norman Teal forgot seven umbrellas in a week and recognised an eighth, which belonged to Frank.
  • In January 1985, Marie dictated an address like a final letter; Elsie only asked for the number.
  • In July 1986, Dolores Van Pelt inaugurated the new ride ledger, with fares starting at $6.
